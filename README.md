# ConsentScan

ConsentScan is a premium, automated cookie consent auditor engineered to analyze compliance tracking across any website. It bridges the gap between privacy regulations and web operations by providing instantly actionable consent health reviews in a single click.

[View Website](https://consentscan-three.vercel.app/)

---

## Key Features & Visual Walkthrough

### 🔍 Automated Compliance Auditing
Instantly scan any web URL to examine your site’s immediate privacy behavior. ConsentScan visually captures banner placements, analyzes button hierarchies, and maps out hidden privacy policies to ensure compliance with global data standard policies.

### 🍪 Advanced Cookie Lifecycle Tracking
See exactly what happens under the hood. The system tracks, logs, and categorizes tracking activities by analyzing what files drop onto a user's machine **before** they interact with a banner, and how those scripts modify **after** a user explicitly grants or denies consent.

### 📁 Workspace Organization & Deep Exporting
Keep your client audits structured by organizing your scans into custom, named regional profiles or corporate project folders. Once your audit is complete, instantly download comprehensive data breakdowns directly to organized Excel spreadsheets for documentation or client handoffs.
